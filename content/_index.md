## About AI Across the Stack

AI Across the Stack is an annual research workshop bringing together PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and senior researchers, primarily from Danish research institutions, alongside invited European speakers.

The workshop explores how advances across AI models, software systems, and computing infrastructure can jointly improve efficiency and sustainability.

## Research Themes

- Efficient AI training and inference
- Models, systems, and infrastructure co-design
- Measuring efficiency and sustainability
- Trade-offs between quality, performance, resources, and environmental impact

## Workshop Format

Rather than following a traditional sequence of research talks, AI Across the Stack combines invited perspectives, concise research presentations, and interactive discussions.

Participants work together to identify open challenges, promising research directions, and opportunities for collaboration.

## AI Across the Stack 2027

The inaugural edition is planned for Copenhagen in 2027, with approximately 20–25 participants.
